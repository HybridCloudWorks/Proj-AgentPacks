# Worked example: what has the team decided about data retention?

Synthetic scenario; the pages and figures are invented.

Request: "What has the team decided about data retention? Write up a summary with sources in the Research area."

1. Frame. The question supports a compliance review next month. Audience: the platform and legal leads. Format: research summary.
2. Search plan. notion-research-synthesis runs "data retention", "retention policy", "log retention" and "delete customer data", first across the workspace, then scoped to the Platform and Legal teamspaces. Results include two decision records, a policy page, a migration plan and three meeting notes; one result comes from a connected chat tool.
3. Select and fetch. Decision records and the policy page first (owned, verified), then the migration plan, then meeting notes for context. Each source's title, URL and last-edited date is recorded.
4. Synthesize. The two decision records conflict: an older one keeps logs for 180 days, a newer one reduces it to 90. The policy page still says 180 and was last edited before the newer decision. The summary states both, explains why 90 days appears current (newer, referenced by the migration plan), and lists the stale policy page as an open question for its owner. The chat-tool result is cited with its source system named. A line about common retention practice is labeled general knowledge.
5. Write. The Research area was named, so creating the page there is Level 1. Every mention uses a URL from the fetch results. The Sources section lists all six cited pages with dates.
6. Verify. The page is fetched; the pre-publication check reports no errors.

Report: COMPLETE_VERIFIED for the page, with its URL. `sources` separates workspace pages, the connected-source result and general knowledge. `followups`: ask the policy owner to reconcile the stale page; the agent did not edit it.
