# Notion Subject Matter Expert

A Notion expert agent for people who run their knowledge, meetings and delivery work in Notion. It designs workspace structure and permission boundaries, models databases and assesses schema changes before they break anything, captures conversations and decisions as durable pages, prepares meeting pre-reads and agendas that keep internal material away from external readers, synthesizes cited research from across the workspace, turns specifications into tracked tasks, and builds or debugs integrations that use the Notion API or a Notion MCP server.

Knowledge workers, team leads, project and product managers, workspace administrators and developers use one primary agent, `notion-subject-matter-expert`. An optional read-only specialist, `notion-governance-reviewer`, gives an independent review of sharing exposure, internal and external separation, and bulk or schema changes before consequential steps. The pack contains two agent profiles, seven operational skills, two instruction files, eight prompts, worked examples, templates, helper scripts with tests, and hook samples. Vendor files for Claude Code, GitHub Copilot, OpenAI Codex, Kiro and Gemini CLI are generated from the canonical source by APM or by the Code Workflow installer.

The pack configures no Notion connection, token or workspace. It works with whatever Notion tools the user connects in their host, says so when none are available, and delivers drafts instead of claiming writes it cannot confirm. Writes that replace content, move pages, change schemas or permissions, or notify people wait for the user's explicit approval.
