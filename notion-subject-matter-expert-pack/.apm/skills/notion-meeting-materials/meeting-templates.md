# Meeting templates

## Internal pre-read

Title: "INTERNAL - <topic> pre-read - <YYYY-MM-DD>". First line: "Internal only. Do not forward to external participants."

1. Meeting at a glance: date, time and time zone, attendees, purpose, the outcome needed.
2. Background: what this is about and why it matters now, with links to the source pages.
3. Where things stand: status, recent changes, relevant numbers, open risks, each cited.
4. Options: for each, what it is, benefits, costs, risks.
5. Recommendation and rationale, if the user asked for one.
6. Questions we need answered and positions to agree before the meeting.
7. General context: frameworks or practices from general knowledge, labeled as such.
8. Link to the agenda.

## Agenda by meeting type

Every agenda opens with: title "<topic> - agenda - <YYYY-MM-DD>", date, time and time zone, duration, attendees, and a one- or two-sentence objective. It ends with an empty "Action items" section and "Related resources" that every reader can open.

| Meeting type | Body |
|---|---|
| Decision | The decision to make; options with a neutral summary; discussion (timed); decision and owner; next steps |
| Status | Progress since last time; upcoming work; blockers and help needed; discussion; next steps |
| Planning | Goal for the period; capacity; candidate work; commitments; risks; next steps |
| Brainstorm | Problem statement; constraints; idea generation (timed); grouping; selection; next steps |
| Retrospective | What went well; what did not; causes; agreed changes with owners |
| One-to-one | Updates from each person; topics; support needed; follow-ups. Private to the two participants |
| External | Introductions; their goals and questions; our content (demo, proposal, review); open questions; agreed next steps |

Timing: give each item minutes, keep five minutes for next steps, and keep the total within the scheduled length.

## Post-meeting capture

Add to the agenda page, or a linked notes page:

1. Decisions: each with the rationale and the owner.
2. Action items: checkbox, owner, due date. Create task pages only when asked.
3. Key outcomes and open questions carried forward.
4. Link to the next meeting in the series, if scheduled.

## Meeting series

A parent page per recurring meeting: purpose, cadence, standing agenda, a linked view of its meetings sorted by date, and an action-item tracker filtered to open items. Individual meeting pages link to the parent.

## When to prepare

- Major decision or external meeting: start several days ahead; share the agenda at least a day before.
- Routine meetings: prepare the day before.
- Short notice: a brief agenda and a short pre-read with only the essentials and the known gaps.
