# Prepare or follow up a meeting

## Inputs

The meeting's purpose, date, time zone, duration, attendees (marking external ones), related project and where meeting documents live. Ask for the purpose if it is unclear; it decides the structure.

## Workflow

Apply notion-meeting-materials, with notion-research-synthesis for background when needed. Draft the internal pre-read and the agenda, keep internal material and the pre-read's title and link out of anything external readers see, and run the pre-publication check on the agenda. For high-stakes external meetings, ask notion-governance-reviewer for a separation review. Do not mention or assign attendees unless asked. After the meeting, capture decisions and action items.

## Expected output

The pre-read and agenda URLs (or drafts), how they link, what each audience can see, and the completion report from the `notion-subject-matter-expert-contract` instruction.
