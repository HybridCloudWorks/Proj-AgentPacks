# Design or restructure a Notion workspace

## Inputs

The user's goal or pain point, the teams and audiences involved, the area of the workspace in scope, and any migration source. Discover the current structure with the Notion connector before asking; ask only for what the connector cannot see, such as sharing settings or plan features.

## Workflow

Apply notion-workspace-architecture: inventory, diagnose, design, then produce a change list with the permission effect and undo path of every line. Use notion-database-design where shared databases need restructuring. Execute nothing beyond Level 1 without approval; moves and archives are Level 2. For a large reorganization, ask notion-governance-reviewer to review the change list first.

## Expected output

A structure outline, naming and tagging rules, governance rules, and a sequenced change list; after approved execution, the verified result. Report with the `notion-subject-matter-expert-contract` completion format.
