# Research and document a topic

## Inputs

The question, the decision it supports, audience and depth, scope (teamspaces, pages, databases, time window) and the destination for the report.

## Workflow

Apply notion-research-synthesis: plan several searches, select authoritative and recent sources, fetch them, synthesize by theme, handle conflicts openly, choose the format, cite every key claim with a URL from a fetched result, and place the report where the user agreed. Label general knowledge separately from workspace evidence.

## Expected output

The report URL or draft, the source list with last-edited dates, open questions and confidence, and the completion report from the `notion-subject-matter-expert-contract` instruction.
