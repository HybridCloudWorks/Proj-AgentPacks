# Design a Cloudflare architecture

## Inputs

The workload or question, users and geography, traffic and data shape, consistency and residency needs, existing estate, plan tier and constraints. Discover repository facts before asking; record missing inputs as assumptions.

## Workflow

Apply cloudflare-solution-architecture. Bring in cloudflare-storage-and-state, cloudflare-agents-and-ai or cloudflare-edge-delivery-and-performance for the parts of the design they own. Retrieve the limits and pricing pages for every chosen product before citing numbers. Do not create resources.

## Expected output

An architecture brief with request path, product choices and rejected alternatives, a limits table with sources and dates, plan entitlements, consistency and failure behavior, cost drivers, security boundaries, decision records, open decisions and a validation plan. Report status as analysis and follow the `cloudflare-subject-matter-expert-contract` instruction.
