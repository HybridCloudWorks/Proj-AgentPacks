# Course Evaluation and Feedback

Use after a session or when designing a feedback and improvement loop.

Measure more than satisfaction: objective confidence, knowledge-check performance, lab completion, artifact quality, transfer intent, accessibility, pacing, and environment friction. Keep survey questions actionable and privacy-conscious. Correlate feedback with objective and activity evidence without identifying learners unnecessarily. Record what changed for the next delivery, what requires courseware-owner clarification, and what is merely a preference. Never claim learning mastery from attendance or a positive survey alone.
