# Cost, Allocation, and Governance Review

Require stated recurring and one-time cost assumptions, owner, cost center, environment, region, SKU/tier, utilization, storage/log growth, backup, egress, private connectivity, and shared-service allocation. Compare the change with any documented budget ceiling, including the USD 150/month ceiling when that repository contract applies.

Check mandatory tags and durable allocation identifiers, budgets and alerts, quota impact, idle/non-production behavior, retention, autoscaling, commitments, and sustainability trade-offs. Do not invent pricing; mark estimates and require current pricing evidence when material.

Review policy exceptions for owner, rationale, scope, expiration, compensating control, and removal plan. A deny policy without a legitimate exception path is a delivery risk; an unbounded exception is governance failure.
