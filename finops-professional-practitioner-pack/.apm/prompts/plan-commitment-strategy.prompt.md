# Plan commitment strategy

## Inputs

60 to 90 days of eligible usage, the current commitment portfolio, planned usage changes, Finance constraints and approval thresholds.

## Workflow

Apply rate-optimization. Confirm usage optimization is not pending, find the steady floor, review utilization and expirations before buying, model coverage, term, payment and flexibility options with commitment savings computed from ContractedCost and EffectiveCost, show the downside, and recommend a tranche with exact specification. Ask finops-evidence-reviewer to check the savings math before any purchase decision.

## Expected output

A commitment recommendation following the skill's template, a portfolio health summary, the approvals required, and the completion report from the `finops-professional-practitioner-contract` instruction. Purchasing, exchanging or letting commitments lapse is Level 2.
