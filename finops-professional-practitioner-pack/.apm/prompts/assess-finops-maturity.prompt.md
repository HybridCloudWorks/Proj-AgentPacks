# Assess FinOps maturity

## Inputs

The spend profile, the artifacts that exist, the people or written answers available, the sponsor and the audience, and any previous assessment.

## Workflow

Apply finops-maturity-assessment. Collect artifact, data and interview evidence per capability, score each separately with confidence, set targets from business value, weigh gaps by exposure, risk, effort and dependency, pick quick wins, and review draft scores with capability owners. Hand each gap to the skill that covers it.

## Expected output

The scorecard, top gaps, quick wins, a 90-day plan with owners and measures, a 12-month roadmap, what was not assessed, and the completion report from the `finops-professional-practitioner-contract` instruction. Distributing the assessment is Level 2.
