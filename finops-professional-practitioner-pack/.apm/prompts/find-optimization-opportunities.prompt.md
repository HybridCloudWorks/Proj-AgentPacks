# Find optimization opportunities

## Inputs

The billing scope, resource-level billing data for at least 30 days, utilization metrics covering business peaks, ownership data and constraints such as service-level objectives, retention rules and change windows.

## Workflow

Apply workload-optimization. Join billing and utilization, require two signals per candidate, record exemptions, classify by confidence, prioritize by impact and effort, apply the pattern rules, write each recommendation with owner, range, trade-off, reversibility and approval level, and plan execution with notice, backup and rollback. Route commitment-coupled resources to rate-optimization. Ask finops-evidence-reviewer to check the savings figures before they are reported.

## Expected output

An optimization inventory, execution plans for the owners, a realized-savings tracking plan with day-normalized baselines, and the completion report from the `finops-professional-practitioner-contract` instruction. Every change to resources is Level 2.
