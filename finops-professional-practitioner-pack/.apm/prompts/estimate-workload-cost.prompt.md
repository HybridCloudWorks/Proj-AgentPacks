# Estimate workload cost

## Inputs

The proposed design or change, the usage profile at steady state and peak, data volumes and growth, regions and availability targets, and any commitments or negotiated rates that apply.

## Workflow

Apply forecasting-and-unit-economics for the estimate and workload-optimization for the architecture review. Price each component by service category at low, expected and high usage, make data transfer explicit, include storage growth, observability, support and licensing, show the managed-service premium, price at on-demand and effective rates, and compare one or two alternatives at 1x, 10x and 100x load. For migrations, budget the overlap period and set the source exit date. Cite the pricing pages and the date they were read.

## Expected output

An estimate table by service category with P10, P50 and P90, alternatives with their cost, speed, quality and carbon trade-offs, the assumptions that move the estimate most, and the completion report from the `finops-professional-practitioner-contract` instruction.
