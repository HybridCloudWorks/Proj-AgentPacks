# Draft a FinOps governance artifact

## Inputs

The artifact needed (policy, cadence, onboarding gate, tool evaluation, enablement plan or integration charter), its scope, its owner, existing documents and the adjacent teams affected.

## Workflow

Apply finops-practice-governance. Keep policies to one page with a checkable rule, an enforcement point, an exception path with expiry and a review date. Make cadences produce decisions with owners. Make onboarding gates yes or no checks. Base tool evaluations on capability gaps with common criteria. Respect the ownership of adjacent disciplines.

## Expected output

The drafted artifact, the open decisions for its owner, the people who must review it, and the completion report from the `finops-professional-practitioner-contract` instruction. Publishing, enforcing or communicating it is Level 2.
