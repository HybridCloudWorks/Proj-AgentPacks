## 1. Scope and cost basis

- Providers, billing accounts and periods covered.
- Cost column: EffectiveCost for allocation; BilledCost for reconciliation to the invoice.
- Filters: usage and purchases included or excluded, corrections handled as a separate line, tax and credits treatment.

## 5. Kubernetes and other shared platforms

Node cost split by requests and by usage; idle and system capacity shown as its own line; namespace and label mapping to the taxonomy.

## 6. Unallocated spend

How it is shown, the current share, the remediation owner and the target date.

## 7. Cadence and true-ups

Report frequency, close-calendar alignment, true-up rule and rounding policy.

## 8. Disputes

How a team raises a dispute, the response time, who decides, where resolutions are published.

## 9. Maturity and next step

Current stage (showback, soft chargeback, hard chargeback), entry criteria for the next stage and their status.
