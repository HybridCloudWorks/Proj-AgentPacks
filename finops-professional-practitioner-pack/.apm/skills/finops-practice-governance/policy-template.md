# FinOps policy (one page)

## Policy

- Name and version:
- Purpose: the outcome the policy protects, in one sentence.
- Scope: providers, accounts, environments and teams covered; what is explicitly excluded.
- Rule: the requirement, stated so it can be checked yes or no.
- Enforcement point: preventive at creation (policy-as-code, pipeline check), or detective after the fact (report, review).
- Exception process: how to request, who decides, maximum duration.
- Owner:
- Review date:
- Related policies:

## Exception register

| Exception | Requested by | Reason | Approved by | Scope (accounts, resources) | Expires | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

Every exception expires. Review the register at the quarterly strategic review; renew deliberately or close.

## Measures

- Compliance rate and its source query or report.
- Attempted violations blocked by preventive enforcement.
- Open and expired exceptions.
