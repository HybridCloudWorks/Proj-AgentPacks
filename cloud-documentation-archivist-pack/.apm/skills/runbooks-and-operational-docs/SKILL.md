# Runbooks and Operational Documentation

Describe trigger, scope, severity, owner, prerequisites, access, safety checks, decision points, commands, expected results, escalation, rollback, recovery, verification, cleanup, and follow-up. The operator should know when to stop and who can authorize the next action.

Use observable symptoms and evidence rather than guessed causes. Include safe diagnostics before mutation. Document stateful systems, backups, data loss risk, maintenance windows, concurrency, and customer impact. Keep credentials and sensitive identifiers out of examples.

Review runbooks after incidents, changes, and successful exercises. Mark untested procedures as untested. Link the runbook to architecture, alerts, dashboards, ADRs, change records, and postmortems without duplicating their content.
