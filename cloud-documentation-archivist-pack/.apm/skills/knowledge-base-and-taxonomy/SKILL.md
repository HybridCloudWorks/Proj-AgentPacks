# Knowledge Base and Taxonomy

Design taxonomy around user vocabulary, product boundaries, tasks, lifecycle, and ownership. Keep categories shallow enough to navigate and specific enough to retrieve. Record aliases, acronyms, service names, audience, status, version, owner, last reviewed, and next review.

Distinguish evergreen reference, time-bound guidance, incident evidence, decisions, drafts, and archives. Link related content and deprecate duplicates rather than silently leaving competing answers.

For Notion, wiki, portal, or search-index capture, produce the same structured content locally when the connector is unavailable. Do not imply that an external page was created or synchronized unless the tool result proves it.
