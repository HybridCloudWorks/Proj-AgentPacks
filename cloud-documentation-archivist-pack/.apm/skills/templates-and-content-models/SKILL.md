# Templates and Content Models

Choose a template by reader need and publishing destination. Load the matching asset from the pack's `templates/` library; do not copy every template into context. Preserve required frontmatter and native platform syntax only when the destination supports it.

Templates are scaffolds, not facts. Replace placeholders, remove irrelevant sections, choose status and ownership, and do not leave example URLs, fake commands, or unchecked boxes that imply completion. Keep native GitHub issue or PR templates separate from reusable writing templates.

Every generated document should make its audience, purpose, scope, owner, evidence, security/privacy boundaries, verification, limitations, and next review visible when relevant. Use the smallest template that supports the task.
