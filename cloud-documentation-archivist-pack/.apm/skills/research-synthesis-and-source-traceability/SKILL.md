# Research and Source Traceability

Define the question, audience, decision or action, date boundary, authoritative sources, search scope, and confidence standard. Prefer primary vendor documentation, standards, source code, and observed evidence; use secondary sources to find leads and label them.

Maintain a source ledger with title, URL or path, publisher, version/date, accessed date, relevant claim, limitations, and whether the source is current. Separate facts, inference, recommendation, unresolved conflict, and not-verified statements.

Synthesize instead of pasting. Preserve material disagreements, distinguish similar product names, check regional/plan/version constraints, and link claims near the evidence. Do not expose private content or publish research to an external system without authorization.
