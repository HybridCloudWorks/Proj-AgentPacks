## Evidence Gaps

For each material gap, name the exact artifact, commit, inputs, target, identity, and risk it must resolve. Do not phrase a gap as a confirmed defect.

## Residual Risk

State only material risk that remains after the reviewed evidence. When no actionable defect is found, use the no-finding language defined in `references/severity-and-output.md`.

## Disposition

Choose one without implying enforcement authority:

- Ready for human approval.
- Ready with conditions.
- Changes requested.
- Blocked by a human or external decision.
- Insufficient evidence for a safe recommendation.
